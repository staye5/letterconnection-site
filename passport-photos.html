2-by-2 U.S. passport photo

We frame and print a passport-size photo for your application. Final acceptance is determined by the U.S. Department of State.

What to wear

Wear normal clothing, remove glasses, and avoid uniforms or camouflage. Religious head coverings worn daily are allowed if the full face is visible.

Easy Stafford location

Find us at the end of Quailridge Plaza, opposite O’Reilly Auto Parts, in Suite B-1. Passport photos, document copies and shipping are available at the same store.

Before you come in

Make the first photo work.

  1. Bring your requirements — tell us if your application needs a format other than the standard U.S. passport photo.
  2. Come photo-ready — neutral expression, eyes open, and no glasses.
  3. Check official requirements — review the U.S. Department of State photo rules for special cases.
