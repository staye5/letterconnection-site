Before you come in

Three things that make it quick.

1 · Call first

Notary availability varies day to day. A quick call confirms someone can stamp your document today and what it will cost.

2 · Don’t pre-sign

The notary must watch you sign. If the document is already signed, it may need to be redone — bring it unsigned.

3 · Bring a photo ID

A current government-issued photo ID (driver’s license, passport, or state ID) is required for every signer.

Questions

Notary FAQ.

How much does notarization cost?

Fees are posted at the counter and follow Texas notary rules — call 281-499-6245 for the current fee for your document type.

Do I need to bring my own witnesses?

For most single-signer documents, no. Some documents (like wills or certain POAs) require witnesses beyond the notary — mention your document type when you call and we’ll tell you exactly what to bring.

Can you notarize for someone who isn’t present?

No — every signer must appear in person with a valid photo ID. That’s a Texas requirement, not a store policy.

Can you fax my notarized document somewhere afterward?

Yes — we can fax or scan-and-email it right from the counter once it’s stamped, so it’s one trip.
