Before you come in

Three things make it quick.

  1. Call first — notary availability varies during the day. One call means the right person is at the counter when you arrive.
  2. Don’t sign in advance — the notary must watch you sign. Bring the document unsigned.
  3. Bring a valid photo ID — every person signing needs to be present with government-issued ID.
Notary questions

Good to know.

How much does notarization cost?

Notary fees are posted at the counter and follow Texas state limits. Call 281-499-6245 and we’ll quote it for your document before you drive over.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment — but call first. Availability varies through the day, and one quick call guarantees a notary is in when you arrive.

Can you notarize for my business?

Yes — business agreements, vendor paperwork, and LLC documents are common. Many local businesses combine this with a business mailbox here.

What if my document needs witnesses?

Some documents require witnesses in addition to the notary. Check your document’s requirements and bring your own witnesses if needed — call us if you’re unsure.
