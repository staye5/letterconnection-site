What we do

One counter for mail, packages & errands.

Since 1984, Letter Connection has been the neighborhood spot in Stafford for a real mailing address and the everyday shipping, printing and document help most people would otherwise drive all over town for.

Private mailboxes

A real Stafford street address with 24/7 lobby access — for your business, for travel, or just to keep your home address off packages and paperwork. Add authorized recipients so family or staff can pick up too.

Package receiving

We accept and sign for deliveries from USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL and Amazon and hold them safely at the counter — so nothing sits on your porch. Great for online sellers and anyone who’s tired of missed deliveries.

Print, copy & scan

Everyday black-and-white or color copies, scans to email, and print jobs. Email your file ahead to admin@letterconnection.com and we’ll have it ready when you arrive.
