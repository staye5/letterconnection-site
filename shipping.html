Questions

Shipping FAQ.

Can I buy a shipping label at the counter?

Call first — 281-499-6245 — and we’ll confirm same-day label options and pricing for your destination before you come in.

Which carriers can I drop off for?

Prepaid USPS, UPS, FedEx and DHL packages are all accepted at the counter and go out with each carrier’s regular pickup.

Do you sell boxes and packing material?

Yes — boxes, padded envelopes, tape and cushioning are available at the counter, priced individually.

Can you pack fragile items for me?

Bring it in and we’ll help you pack it properly with the right cushioning. Supplies are charged at counter prices.
