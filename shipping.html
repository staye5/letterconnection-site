Shipping questions

Good to know.

Where can I drop off a UPS package in Stafford?

Bring a labeled prepaid UPS package to Letter Connection at 435 Murphy Rd, Suite B-1 in Quailridge Plaza during staffed hours. We can also help with eligible paid UPS shipments and packing.

Where can I drop off a FedEx package in Stafford?

Bring an eligible prepaid FedEx Express, Ground or Ground Economy package to our Stafford counter during staffed hours.

Can you print my FedEx QR label?

Yes. Bring your supported FedEx QR code and package to our counter. We scan the code and print the label at no extra charge with your eligible prepaid drop-off. Packing and new postage are separate.

Can I drop off an Amazon return here?

If Amazon gave you a printable prepaid carrier label (USPS, UPS, FedEx or DHL), yes — box it, label it, and drop it at the counter. We also print supported FedEx QR labels. Follow the return method selected with your retailer; a code assigned to another return network may require a different location.

Can you pack my shipment and print a label?

Yes. Printing a supported FedEx QR label is included with your eligible drop-off. Printing an existing emailed or PDF carrier label is $0.90. Eligible prepaid drop-offs are free. New postage, packing labor and materials are separate; we explain the price before proceeding.

When do carriers pick up?

We accept eligible drop-offs throughout store hours, with no separate hard customer cutoff. Carrier collection times determine when a package begins moving. For a time-sensitive shipment, ask us about the next collection and available services.

Where is Letter Connection?

We are at the end of Quailridge Plaza, opposite O’Reilly Auto Parts: 435 Murphy Rd, Suite B-1, Stafford, TX 77477.

Package ready to go?

Bring it by during store hours — or call about the next carrier collection.
