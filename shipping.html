Shipping questions

Good to know.

Can I drop off Amazon returns?

If Amazon gave you a prepaid carrier label (USPS, UPS, FedEx or DHL), yes — box it, label it, and drop it at the counter. Label-free QR-code returns depend on the carrier’s program — call and we’ll confirm.

Can I buy a shipping label here?

Call first for paid label pricing — 281-499-6245. For prepaid packages, just walk in.

When do carriers pick up?

Each carrier sweeps the counter on its own schedule, most business days. Shipping something time-sensitive? Call and we’ll tell you the cutoff for that carrier today.

Will you pack it for me?

We stock boxes and supplies and we’re glad to help you pack it right at the counter.

Package ready to go?

Bring it by — or call about today’s carrier cutoffs.
