How it works

Three steps to an easier pickup.

  1. Email your file to admin@letterconnection.com — include color or B&W, paper size, single/double-sided, copies and preferred pickup time.
  2. We confirm the price and timing, print the agreed job and reply when it is ready.
  3. Pick it up at the counter and pay there. That’s it.
Printing questions

Good to know.

Can you help with repeat printing for my business?

Yes. Send your current file, quantity and deadline for forms, flyers, menus or other everyday documents. We confirm the job price and pickup time. No membership or design package is required to place a print order.

What does printing cost?

Standard printing is 99¢ per page. We confirm your job total and pickup time before starting. Call 281-499-6245 to discuss larger jobs or special requirements.

What file types can you print?

PDF is best. Word documents and images work too — if something looks off, we’ll call you before printing.

Do you do passport photos?

Call ahead for passport photos — availability varies, and one call makes sure we can take them the day you come in.

Can you print shipping labels?

Yes — email your existing prepaid label and we’ll print it for $0.90. Eligible prepaid package drop-offs are free. New postage and packing are separate.

Have a file to print?

Email it now — pick it up on your next errand run.
