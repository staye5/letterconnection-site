Printing FAQ.

How much do copies and prints cost?

Pricing is posted at the counter — call 281-499-6245 for a quick quote, especially for color or large jobs.

Do you do passport photos?

What file types can you print?

PDF is best. Word documents, JPG/PNG images, and most email attachments are fine too. If it opens, we can usually print it.
