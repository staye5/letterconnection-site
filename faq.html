Questions & answers

Good to know.

The things people ask most before opening a mailbox. Don’t see yours? Call the counter at 281-499-6245 or email admin@letterconnection.com.

How much does a mailbox cost?

Choose 3 months for $58.46 total, 6 months for $103.92 total, or our most popular 12-month plan for $194.85 total. These totals include tax, setup and your first key. Rent is $54, $96 or $180 before tax, paid upfront. A one-month plan is also available through the store for $21.65 including tax. Existing customers retain their agreed renewal terms. Compare plans.

Is this a real street address or a PO box?

It is a real street-style mailing address — 435 Murphy Rd Ste B-1, Stafford, TX 77477 — with your assigned mailbox number. We receive mail and packages from USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL and Amazon.

What do I need to bring to open one?

We will help you complete the required mailbox agreement and USPS Form 1583 process. Call before your visit for the identification requirements. Your address is activated after payment verification and required setup.

Do you receive packages from all carriers?

Yes — we accept and sign for USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL and Amazon deliveries and hold them safely at the counter until you pick them up. No more porch theft or missed-delivery slips.

How can I pay?

We take cash, card, check (payable to Talal Technology Group LLC) and Zelle. New mailbox customers can choose 3, 6 or 12 months, review the full signup total and pay securely through Clover, then complete required setup at the store. Existing customers should call for renewal pricing. Callback requests remain free.

Can more than one person pick up my mail?

Yes. You can list authorized recipients on your account — helpful for families, spouses, or business staff — so they can collect mail and packages for the box too.

What are your hours?

Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–6:00 PM; Saturday 10:00 AM–4:30 PM; closed Sunday. Mailbox customers have 24/7 coded lobby access to their mail. After-hours package collection is available by prior arrangement.

Can I use this as my business address?

Many local businesses use their mailbox for business correspondence, invoices and vendor paperwork. Use the assigned address format and confirm any particular filing or listing requirements before relying on it.
