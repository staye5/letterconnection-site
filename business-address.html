Business address questions

Good to know.

Can I use this address for my Texas LLC?

Many of our box holders use their address here on business filings and everyday business paperwork. A real street address is widely accepted where PO Boxes often aren’t — confirm any specific filing’s address rules with your filing agent or attorney.

Is this a “virtual mailbox”?

It’s better for local businesses: a physical box you can open 24/7, with a real person at the counter signing for your packages. No app subscription, no per-scan fees — just $180 a year, posted.

Can my employees or partners pick up mail?

Yes — you can list authorized recipients on your account, and each one is covered by the same USPS Form 1583 process.

What does it cost?

$180 per year (about $15/month) — same posted price for personal or business — plus a one-time $25 setup & key fee. Shorter terms are available; see pricing.

I travel or sell online — will packages pile up safely?

That’s the point. Everything is received, signed for, and held inside — no porch, no theft, no missed-delivery slips. Come collect whenever suits you.
