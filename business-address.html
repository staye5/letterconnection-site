Business address questions

Good to know.

Can I use this address for my Texas LLC?

Our direct mailbox gives your LLC a business mailing address. Texas allows a street address or PO Box for an LLC’s initial mailing address. A registered agent and registered office serve a separate purpose; registered-agent service is not included in this mailbox plan. Use the correct address for each field on your filing. Texas LLC filing instructions.

Will this keep my home address private?

Use your assigned business mailing address on invoices, customer correspondence and other places that accept a mailing address. Renting a mailbox does not remove an address from earlier filings or replace every physical-address requirement. For a new LLC, review the mailing, governing-person and registered-office fields before filing.

Is this a “virtual mailbox”?

Our direct mailbox is a physical mailbox rented from Letter Connection, with payment through our Clover checkout and local help at the counter. For app-based remote mail management, we also offer a separate iPostal1 option at this location. Its plans, payment and service allowances are separate from our direct mailbox.

Can my employees or partners pick up mail?

Yes — you can list authorized recipients on your account, and each one is covered by the same USPS Form 1583 process.

What does it cost?

$180 per year (about $15/month), with setup and your first key included, plus $14.85 tax: $194.85 initially for a new annual standard mailbox. Existing customers retain their agreed renewal terms. All three signup options are on the pricing page.

I travel or sell online — will packages pile up safely?

We receive and hold packages at the store. Tell us ahead of time if you expect a large item, extended storage or an after-hours pickup. For collection after closing, wait for confirmation that your package has been staged.
